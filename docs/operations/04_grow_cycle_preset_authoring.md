# Grow Cycle Preset Authoring & Recipe Customization

## Stage Definitions
- **Germination / Seedling**: Lower EC (0.6 - 1.0 mS/cm), balanced pH (5.8 - 6.2).
- **Vegetative**: Moderate EC (1.2 - 1.8 mS/cm), robust nitrogen uptake.
- **Flowering / Bloom**: High EC (1.8 - 2.4 mS/cm), elevated phosphorus/potassium ratio.
- **Flush**: Clean water baseline, zero nutrient additions.
